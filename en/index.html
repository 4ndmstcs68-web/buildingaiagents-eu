07  Frequently asked

What people usually ask first.

Does our data stay with us?

Yes. You choose where the agent runs and which model it uses — that is not my call. If your data is sensitive, we use European hosting or a model that runs entirely on your own server. It costs a bit more, but then literally nothing leaves the building.

We're not technical. Can we do this?

That is the starting point, not the problem. On the build track you do nothing technical — you get something that works. In the training there is usually nobody in the room with a programming background. Can someone at your place put together a formula in Excel? Then this works too.

What if it doesn't work for us?

That is what the Scan is for. If it turns out an agent won't return much in your situation, I say so and it stops there. I'd rather have a client who spent €1,950 and walks away happy than one who paid €6,500 for something that sits still.

How long does a project take?

A Scan: about a week. A first working agent: two to six weeks. The difference is almost always in how many systems it has to hang off and how fast I get access — that last one is usually the real delay.

What about the EU AI Act?

For what most SMEs do — admin, follow-up, handling documents — you fall outside the high-risk categories. During the Scan we go through what applies to you and what you should keep on file. No scare story, but it's on paper.

Do you work outside Belgium?

Yes. I prefer to give training on site in the Benelux — a day together in one room simply works better. Build projects run just as well remotely. Dutch or English, your choice.
